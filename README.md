A make.fun entry for "One Line, One Laugh" — one funny line, at most 140 characters, short enough to read in a breath and whole enough to land on its own.

## The entry

> My robot vacuum applied to NASA to explore Mars, but its dustbin fits one crumb and it panics at rugs.

102 characters, 1 line. A robot vacuum with big ambitions and a small dustbin: it dreams of exploring Mars, but it can hold a single crumb and is terrified of rugs.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 140 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
